Modern Web Browsers

Today’s browsers offer many of the same basic tools, but they also have different features.

Popular Options

Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge are common choices. They can all open websites, save bookmarks, and manage tabs. Their differences include browser engines, device integration, and privacy tools.

What makes a good browser?

  • Speed: Pages load and respond quickly.
  • Security: Features help protect users from unsafe websites.
  • Privacy: Settings help control tracking and stored information.
  • Accessibility: Websites and controls are easier for more people to use.
  • Compatibility: Modern websites display and work as expected.

No browser is perfect for everyone. The best option depends on the device and what a person values most.
